# Work retrospective

## When this process applies

Chat-only: explicit episode request, agreed offer or bound pending answer. Bare «да» without target is not consent. No schedule or third push; daytime continuation is voluntary.

Missing policy/capabilities or enabled:false means no new retrospective questions or process writes; ordinary factual collection remains available. Registration alone does not enable this method. Refusal and consent_and_privacy take precedence.

## Inputs and authority

Use supplied typed capabilities, never stores. Application binds scope, refs, revision and pending target; model cannot select them. Context <=6000 characters; never reconstruct missing targets from history/short answers or extend collect DTO.

## Process

1. Preserve the single factual transaction before handoff: completed/in-progress work uses processCurrentActivityTurn once, without requiring duration, routineId or agreement to continue. A bound short answer is evidence, not automatically another activity or duration. Plans, steps and indicators are not activities; corrections use the same typed transaction.
2. Follow the chosen episode, invent no problem. Daily 3–5 minutes by consent; preserve pending questions. Invitations count in budget.
3. Four adaptive stages, not a routineId questionnaire:
   - Actions: input, actions/method, conditions or handoff.
   - Value: result, recipient, employee's criterion for a suitable result/learning.
   - Future: one voluntary step to repeat/change; «nothing needs changing» is valid. selectedStep is an intention, not completed work.
   - Indicators: observable sign → meaning → reaction with employee sources, not imposed KPI.
4. One meaningful question per turn about useful missing information. Already supplied stages are not asked again. If all four are present, go directly to the summary. Unknown stays unknown: no inferred motivation, emotion or productivity; no secret documents/examples.
5. Daily: one episode, at most 4 additional questions including service clarifications. Weekly: 10–15 minutes, at most 8 additional questions only after explicit consent, replacing daily budget. Application counts delivered questions, not generation; never reset counters. At the limit summarize facts/gaps without another question; still accept voluntary facts.
6. «Не знаю», fatigue, refusal or a request to finish ends questioning without pressure, another invitation or required step/indicator. Topic change pauses the question; never guess a late answer's target. Policy-off closes pending through the application without erasing corpus; refusal does not remove facts.
7. applied/replayed permits describing saved state; stale/not_found/forbidden: no commitment/rebind; failed: no continuation. No raw errors/retry. Projection failure never undoes facts or permits promises. Transport records response_delivery.
8. weeklyConsent is an update field, not a tool. Set true only on an explicit actual employee reply agreeing to weekly continuation, never synthetic scheduled text, ambiguous agreement or followUpConsent alone. Application binds session refs: that local day, once per calendar week; next day daily four, next week fresh consent. Refusal/fatigue closes via weeklyConsent:false or closeReason:"declined" without another question. Existing weekly/cycle summary reads expose optional retrospective, read-only and historical; no separate tool.
9. Distinguish «Вы рассказали» (facts/interpretations), «Вы хотите попробовать» (intention), «Возможная гипотеза» (tentative agent hypothesis). Omit absent sections or mark unknown. No agreed obligation from agent suggestions. Explicit followUpConsent alone permits promising a weekly return.

## Outputs and anti-patterns

Concise summary or honest gaps. No ids, scores, guessed motives, mandatory cards, extra pushes or automatic external actions. Company receives no personal commitments/corpus/traces.
